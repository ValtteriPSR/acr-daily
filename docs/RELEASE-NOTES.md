## ACR Rally Club 0.2.3

- Rally schedules now list special stages in driving order, with route names, weather, in-game time and service before each stage. Initial service is shown even for older configurations without a first-stage service flag.
- Compact Conditions cards show damage, wear, failures, penalties, respawn and the actual fixed launcher settings for starting position, grip, wetness, snow cover and time progression.
- Recovery-key login, registration and legacy linking have been removed. Players sign in through Steam and can continue editing their club nickname. Existing Steam accounts, rally entries and results are preserved.
- Polish and English views, player documentation and portable hosting versions are updated.

The downloadable installer still targets https://acr-rally-club.onrender.com. Existing compatible launchers remain supported. Steam verification and Discord delivery are mocked in automated tests; no player account is used for production validation.
